Privacy Policy

  • No account. Photo Cleaner does not require sign-up and operates without any account.
  • On-device analysis. Duplicate detection, similarity, blur checks and size verification run entirely on your iPhone. No photo or library metadata is uploaded to us.
  • No servers, no tracking. We run no photo server, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK. We do not sell or share data.
  • iCloud is Apple's service. When you ask the app to retrieve originals, the download goes through Apple's Photos/iCloud frameworks. The app cannot and does not read your Apple ID.
  • Your files stay yours. Exported copies are written to the app's document folder on your device. Deleting the app removes its local files. Photos you delete through the app are handled by the system Photos confirmation and may appear in Recently Deleted.
  • Diagnostics. The optional diagnostics export contains only app/system versions and task counts — never filenames, asset identifiers or image content.
  • Children. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

隐私政策

  • 无需账号。本应用不需要注册，也不依赖任何账号运行。
  • 本机分析。重复检测、相似照片、模糊检测与大小核实全部在你的 iPhone 上完成。照片和图库元数据不会上传给我们。
  • 无服务器、无追踪。我们没有照片服务器，不集成统计 SDK 或广告 SDK，不出售或共享数据。
  • iCloud 属于苹果服务。当你主动取回原片时，下载通过苹果照片/iCloud 框架完成。应用无法读取你的 Apple ID。
  • 文件属于你。导出的副本保存在本机应用文档目录；卸载应用会删除其本地文件。通过应用删除的照片由系统照片确认流程处理，可能进入「最近删除」。
  • 诊断信息。可选诊断导出仅包含应用/系统版本与任务数量，绝不包含文件名、资源标识或图像内容。
  • 儿童。本应用不收集任何人的个人数据，包括儿童。
